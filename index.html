Product Strategy & Execution Learnings

Product Touchpoint Preview FT.com Article UI
01 • Core Insight

Why the "Mix" habit wins

Behavioural analysis revealed that subscribers who rely exclusively on onsite browsing or exclusively on email digests plateau early. Those who combine onsite topic discovery with offsite alert re-entry achieve the highest median RFV (Recency, Frequency, Volume).

02 • Solving alert fatigue

Precision over volume

Previous analysis before we started saw 43% of newsletter subscribers receiving 100+ emails in month one. By consolidating legacy Author, Keyword, and B2B alerts into unified myFT digests with send-time optimisation, we lifted open rates (+3%) and CTR (+5%) while smoothing peak email infrastructure load, and reducing Total Cost of Ownership in our tech estate.

03 • Curation + Algorithms

Editorial authority at scale

We tested behavioural ("Because you followed..."), popularity, and demographic recommenders alongside human-curated FT Collections, which delivered a +3% engagement uplift by pairing editorial judgement with personalised topic graphs.
