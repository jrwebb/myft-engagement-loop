01 • Core Insight

Why the "Mix" Habit Wins

Behavioral analysis revealed that subscribers who rely exclusively on onsite browsing or exclusively on email digests plateau early. Readers who combine onsite topic discovery with offsite alert re-entry achieve the highest median RFV (Recency, Frequency, Volume).

02 • Solving Alert Fatigue

Precision Over Volume

Early cohorts saw 43% of newsletter subscribers receiving 100+ emails in month one. By consolidating legacy Author, Keyword, and B2B alerts into unified myFT digests with send-time optimization, we lifted open rates (+3%) and CTR (+5%) while smoothing peak email infrastructure load.

03 • Curation + Algorithms

Editorial Authority at Scale

We tested behavioral ("Because you followed..."), popularity, and demographic recommenders alongside human-curated FT Collections—which delivered a +3% engagement uplift by pairing editorial judgment with personalized topic graphs.
